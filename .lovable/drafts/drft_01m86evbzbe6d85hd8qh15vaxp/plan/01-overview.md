# Co-Pilot as a radar icon at the top of the screen

## What changes
- **One radar icon** replaces the Co-Pilot bar at the top of the screen. Click it to open the same text box (same "Ask me anything..." / deal prompt text, same double-click to expand, drag, resize, Esc to close). Nothing about how Co-Pilot works changes.
- **No more coloured squares** (green/orange/red counts) after the "Deal Co-Pilot" label — the Deal Checker chip already shows them.
- **Available at every stage**, not only in execution. The "Co-Pilot >" button in set-up goes away; the icon replaces it.
- **Inside an expanded deal (DTS):** the icon sits at the top of the DTS panel, in its header row, focused on that deal (e.g. "Focus: Meridian Gulf Bank").
- **Deal collapsed / on a menu page:** the icon sits at the top of the page bar, labelled with the current focus — the active screen or panel (e.g. "Focus: New Issue Board", or the last update clicked).
- In execution, the larger Co-Pilot work area in the centre stays as it is today (quick commands, previews, Execute).
