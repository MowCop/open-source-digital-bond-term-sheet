## What gets saved

**Knowledge note: Deal phases and what is public**
- **Set-up (pre-public):** nothing has been or will be sent externally. The only announcement is usually a short text saying the mandate was awarded. The deal terms announcement follows only when the deal launches.
- **Execution (bookbuilding):** starts when the deal launches. Bankers set initial terms, sales desks contact buyers, and orders go "into the book".
- **Public vs private terms during bookbuilding:** some terms are set and public; others are still being discussed. Those stay in the term sheet fields as variable, unpublished values.
  - Example: IPTs / Initial Guidance / Spread Talk (all the same thing) published as MS+75bp +/- 10bp (MS+75 to MS+85).
  - The syndicate may then see a tighter range internally, such as MS+75bp +/- 5bp. This goes into the term sheet marked green, as unpublished.
- **Publishing:** updated terms are only made public in the next stage announcement, once all syndicate members and usually the issuer agree.
